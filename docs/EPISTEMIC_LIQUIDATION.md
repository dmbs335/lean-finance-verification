# Epistemic Liquidation Hypothesis

A research hypothesis extending crowding models:

```text
methodology or evidence shock
        ↓
confidence in strategy validity decreases
        ↓
capital allocation decreases
        ↓
liquidation pressure increases
        ↓
market impact
```

The hypothesis is distinct from ordinary position crowding. The shared exposure is not necessarily the asset position; it is the evidence dependency structure behind strategies.

This module provides formal vocabulary only. Empirical validation requires real strategy populations, dependency graphs, and market event studies.
